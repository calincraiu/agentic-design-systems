# What did your measure change?

<p class="lead">And what does it still not see?</p>

<!-- notes -->
Two or three pairs show their reports and towers side by side, and answer both questions.

The first question is about the evidence: which variant did the new limit rule out or bring in, and do the receipts show it? The second is about the measure's blind side. Every answer to it is either another measure you could build, or something you'll always judge by eye, as a designer. Both are useful to know before you trust an agent's search with a design.
