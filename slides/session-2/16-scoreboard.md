Towers are ranked by floor area. A tower that takes 20% or more of the park's sun, or reaches past the plot, is struck through and doesn't rank, whatever its floor area.

Before you accept the winner, check its receipts the same way you checked your own: the floor area from the tower's receipt, the sun lost from the `park_sun` receipt with the same label.
