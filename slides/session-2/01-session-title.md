# Tools and measures

Grasshopper definitions as tools, and numbers that say what "good" means.

<!-- notes -->
Session 1 built the parts of an agentic system around a tower that only existed as numbers. In this session the tower stands on a real site in Rhino, next to a park and a river. Claude runs Grasshopper definitions as tools, the way it ran `dice.py`, and you decide what counts as a good tower by choosing measures and setting their limits.

You need Rhino 8 or 9 with RhinoAI's MCP server and Ladybug Tools installed. The setup checklist went out before the session.
