# What did your check change?

<p class="lead">And what can't it see?</p>

<!-- notes -->
Two or three pairs show their reports and towers side by side, and answer both questions.

The first question is about evidence: which variant did one of your limits rule out or bring in, and do the receipts show it? The second is about the blind side of your measures. Each answer to it is either another tool you could build, or something you'll always judge by eye, as a designer. Both are worth knowing before you let an agent search for designs on your behalf.
