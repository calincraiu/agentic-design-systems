# What's given, and what's yours

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="bridge" data-start="1"></div>

<!-- notes -->
The runner, the `grasshopper` skill and the finished tower and `park_sun` tools are given. You build three things on top of them: `sum`, the smallest possible tool, `daylit`, a measure, and `tower-check`, a skill that applies your limits.

The runner is the same for all of them. Once it exists, a new tool needs only a definition with named inputs and outputs, and a contract that says what it does. That's why the work you do in Grasshopper today is the part that's yours: what goes in, what the rule is, what comes out, and where you draw the limit.
