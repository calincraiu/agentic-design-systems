# What did no metric capture?

<p class="lead">Call out the one thing you brought.</p>

<!-- notes -->
At the end of Session 1 you wrote down one thing you cared about in the towers that no metric measured. The usual answers are the shadow on the park, the view from the river, terraces and outdoor space, the light left for the neighbours, structure and cost, and the feel at street level.

Some of these become measures in this session, as Grasshopper definitions you can run on any tower. Others stay out of reach of a number, and that is a finding too. Keep your answer at hand: in step 5 you choose the measure closest to it.
