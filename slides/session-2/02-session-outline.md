# Session 2 in five steps

1. **A Grasshopper tool.** Claude asks, Rhino runs it.
2. **Make your own.** A definition and its contract.
3. **The tower on its site.** Same numbers, new engine.
4. **The challenge.** One measure, one scoreboard.
5. **Choose what counts.** Your measure, your limit.

<!-- notes -->
Steps 1 to 3 are about **tools**: a Grasshopper definition that Claude can run with inputs and read outputs from, without anyone opening the canvas. You build a small one yourself. Steps 4 and 5 are about **measures**: tools that take a design and return a number, such as how much sun a park loses. A measure only means something once someone sets a limit on it, and setting that limit is your job.

Most of the session is hands-on. The slides between tasks are short; the detail is in these notes.
