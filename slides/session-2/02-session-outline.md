# Session 2 in four steps

1. **A Grasshopper tool.** Two numbers in, one out, built by you.
2. **A chain on the site.** The tower, a measure, and one you build.
3. **A skill that judges.** Your measures, your limits.
4. **Crit.** What your check changed, and what it can't see.

<!-- notes -->
Each step uses what the one before it made. The adding tool shows what turns a Grasshopper definition into something Claude can run, and checks that your setup works. The tower and its measures use the same runner, at the scale of a building. The skill you write puts your measures and limits together, so the design skill from Session 1 has something to answer to.

Most of the session is hands-on. The slides between tasks are short; the detail is in these notes.
