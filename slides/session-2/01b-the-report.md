# Three towers, judged on your terms

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="report" data-start="1"></div>

<!-- notes -->
This is the kind of report you get at the end of the session. Claude designs three variants of the tower, runs your `tower-check` skill on each, and reports every measure against its limit. Here A passes both, B misses the `daylit` limit and C misses the `park_sun` limit.

Each verdict follows from a limit you set, so a different limit would give a different verdict. The decision stays with you: a variant that passes can still be wrong for reasons no measure sees, and one that fails may be worth keeping if you change the limit and say why.

The values are examples. Your report will have your measures, your limits and your towers.
