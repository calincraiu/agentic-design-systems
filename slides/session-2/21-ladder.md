# Today you wrote tools and measures

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="ladder" data-start="2"></div>

> **Take-home:** the measure you wanted that isn't on the menu. Copy `tools/grasshopper/TEMPLATE.md` and write its **contract**: inputs, the rule in words, outputs, two test cases by hand, and what it can't tell you. No Grasshopper needed.

<!-- notes -->
On the ladder of authorship you climbed two steps today. A **tool** is a fixed rule with named inputs and outputs and a contract, and you built one. A **measure** is a tool that says how good a design is on one count, and only becomes a judgement once someone sets its limit. The limits in your skill are yours.

The take-home is the first half of building a tool: the contract. Writing it forces the decisions that make a measure useful or useless: what goes in (a layer, a city, a number), which unit, what exactly is counted, and what the number can't see. Work out the test cases on paper, on shapes simple enough to check by hand, such as a plain 20 × 20 m box.

Keep your own folder as it is. The course folder is replaced with a fresh copy each session, but your folder isn't touched.
