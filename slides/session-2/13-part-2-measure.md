# Who decides what's good?

<p class="lead">A measure turns "kind to the park" into a number. You decide the limit.</p>

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="measure" data-start="0" data-ghost class="map"></div>

<!-- notes -->
A **measure** is a tool that takes a design and returns a number with a meaning: here, the share of the park's sunshine a tower takes away. On its own the number decides nothing. It becomes a judgement once someone sets a **limit** ("less than 20%"), and that limit belongs in your skill, where you can read it, argue about it and change it. Every measure also has a blind side, written down in its contract under *What it can't tell you*.
