# Choose what counts

1. **Pick** the measure closest to the thing you brought.
2. **Read** its contract, especially *What it can't tell you*.
3. **Paste** its row into your Measures table. **Set your own limit**, and write one sentence on why that number.
4. **New session:** run `/tower-in-rhino` with the Session 1 wish.

> **You're done when** the report shows both measures, and you can say which limit changed the variant it recommends.

<!-- notes -->
The limit is the authorship here. The row in each contract comes with a starting limit, but it's a guess, not a rule. Write yours from what you care about. If you want terraces people can sit on, how deep is a terrace you would sit on? If the river view matters, how much of the tower should a passer-by see, compared with the 1,316 m² of a plain 12-floor tower?

Write your sentence next to the row, in the skill, so whoever reads the skill next knows where the number came from:

```
| tools/terraces/terraces.gh | floors = the tower run's floors layer | largest_terrace_m2 >= 20 |
```
*20 m²: room for a table for six and a planter. (Ours.)*

Compare the three variants with your challenge result. A second limit usually changes which variant passes, and sometimes nothing passes at all. That isn't a failure: it means your wish asks for more than this tower can give, and the skill must say so rather than loosen your limit. It is not allowed to change the table itself.

If your thing isn't on the menu (structure, cost, the feel at street level), choose the nearest measure for today, and keep your thing for the take-home on the last slide.
