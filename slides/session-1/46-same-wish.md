You gave the same wish twice today. At the start, Chat gave you one fluent answer with numbers in it. Now your system has given you a brief, three measured towers and a report on the trade-offs. Compare them row by row:

- **Numbers.** Chat's numbers were written because they fitted the answer. In the gallery, each number was measured by the tool, and rerunning it gives the same result. But the tool only measures what its metrics cover. Its shadow figure is all the ground in shadow, not the shadow on the park, so neither answer actually measured the park.
- **Assumptions.** Check whether Chat said what it assumed about the park, the river and the plot. Your brief has a section for that, with each limit marked "you" or "assumed".
- **Options and choice.** Chat gave one answer. Your system gave three strategies and left the choice to you.

Neither answer tells you whether the tower is good. One of them does show how it was made and what it assumed, and you need that before you can argue about whether it's good.

Find one thing in the Chat answer that you now know was a guess, and one thing your gallery can't tell you either.
