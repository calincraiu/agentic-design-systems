# Write a design stance

1. **Take the tower kit** into your folder (prompt below).
2. Choose a **stance** and name it.
3. Ask Claude to **copy** `tower-designer` to `tower-<your-stance>`.
4. **Rewrite** the *Design stance* section yourselves. Leave the workflow alone.
5. Each run your own stance on **the wish you gave Chat at the start**, in a new session. Answer its questions honestly.

- *Good Neighbour*: the city first
- *Sculptor*: silhouette leads
- *Spreadsheet*: efficiency, always
- *Storyteller*: your words, literally

<!-- notes -->
**The idea.** The tower designer's stance is **Balanced**: it has no strong views. Yours will. A stance says what the skill values, what it refuses, and how its three variants should differ. Write it in your own words: that section is the only part you change.

Running two stances on **the same wish** is the experiment. It's the wish everyone gave Chat at the start, so you can also compare with that answer on the next slide. Same words in, same tool, same workflow, and still different towers come out. The difference is your stance, and that's authorship you can see.

**Along the way:**

- When it stops with questions, answer as yourself, honestly. You're the client.
- Read the brief it writes in `briefs/` and correct anything you disagree with. It's your brief.
- Open `runs/gallery.html` and choose a direction yourself. Claude's recommendation is only an opinion.

**If the gallery labels your run `tower-designer`**, the name in your copied skill wasn't changed. Ask Claude to fix the `name:` line.

**Try it yourself.** Put your two galleries side by side. Where did the same words get read differently?
