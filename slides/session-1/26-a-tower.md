# A tower

<p class="lead">From your words to the tool's numbers, and back.</p>

<!-- notes -->
**In this section.** The same six parts, now on something worth designing. The tower tool takes eight numbers and returns a measured tower. The hard part is the step before that: turning what someone *wants* ("calm, but noticed from the river") into those eight numbers.

You'll play the client for a skill that does that translation. Then you'll write a **design stance** of your own and see how much it changes what comes out.
