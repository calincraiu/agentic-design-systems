# Add the kits

1. **Download** the zip below and **unzip** it.
2. **Copy** the `tools` and `.claude` folders into your own folder.
3. **Check** that both are there.

<!-- notes -->
**The idea.** A **kit** is a small folder of ready-made parts: tools, and the skills that go with them. Copying a kit into your folder is how you add those parts to your own project. The two kits here give you the add and dice tools, and the `add-up` and `tie-breaker` skills.

**How to copy.**

1. Download the zip with the link below the steps, and unzip it. You'll see two folders: `tools` and `.claude`.
2. Open your own folder (the one from the last slide) in File Explorer or Finder, and drag both folders in. If your folder already has a `.claude` folder, merge them: keep everything from both.
3. Check it worked. Inside your folder there should be a `tools` folder with `add.py` and `dice.py` in it, and `.claude/skills/` with `add-up` and `tie-breaker`.

**The `.claude` folder is hidden.** Folders whose names start with a dot are hidden on most computers, so you may not see it. On Windows: in File Explorer, View › Show › Hidden items. On a Mac: in Finder, press Cmd + Shift + . (full stop). It has to be copied too, because that's where the skills live.

**Or copy from the course folder.** The kits are also in the course folder, under `kits`. The widget lists the exact folders on your computer. Copy the *contents* of `kits/add` and `kits/dice` (not the folders themselves) into yours.

**If something goes wrong.** The tools only run from inside your folder, so make sure `tools` is directly in it, not inside another folder from the unzip. Anything you copy is a copy: the originals in the course folder are untouched, so you can always do it again.
