# Eight towers, one tool

<div data-widget="contact" data-images="slim-beacon,quarter-turn,squat-block,leaning,flared-top,long-slab,full-turn,needle"></div>

> Which one is best? **How would you know?**
>
> And if it stands next to a park?

<!-- notes -->
All eight towers came from the same tool, given different numbers for floors, size, twist, taper, lean and so on. The tool has no taste, so any judgement about which one is best has to come from somewhere else.

Words like "elegant", "grounded" or "dramatic" describe real qualities, but they stay opinions until you can say what you would look at to check them. "Grounded" might mean a wide base, a low centre of mass or little overhang, and each of those can be measured.

The second question, about the park, changes what "best" means. A tower can only be better or worse for some purpose, and stating that purpose is what a **brief** does. "Next to a park" also suggests a measurement, the shadow on the grass, though only if the tool knows where the park is. The tower tool doesn't (more on that in Part 2).

Pick your favourite of the eight and write one sentence on why. Underline each word a tool could check. For the words that are left, note what you would need to measure.
