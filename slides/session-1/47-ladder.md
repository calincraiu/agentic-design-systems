<!-- notes -->
The module follows a **ladder of authorship**, six steps from using an agent to designing the system it works in. You started on the bottom step, **prompting**, when you asked Chat for a tower. Today you also wrote **context** (your `CLAUDE.md`) and changed **skills**, including writing a design stance of your own. Session 2 covers **tools**, starting by writing down what a tool of your own should do, and **measures**, which define "good" in numbers. Sessions 3 and 4 connect these into a **workflow** with your decisions built in.

Each step up turns something you would otherwise leave to the model into something written down that you can read, change and test. The question from the start of the session, how you would know whether any of this is good, can only be answered on the upper steps.

Before Session 2, write down one thing you cared about in today's towers that no metric captured, such as a view, the feel at street level or a terrace, and bring it with you.

Keep your own folder as it is, since your `CLAUDE.md`, skills and tools are in it. The course folder is replaced with a fresh copy each session, but your folder isn't touched.
