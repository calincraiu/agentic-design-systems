# The model is far away. Your files are here.

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="where-it-runs"></div>

> Whatever the model reads leaves your laptop: text, images, files.

<!-- notes -->
**The idea.** The model is far too big for a laptop. It runs on large computers in a **data centre**, run by the company that makes it. The app runs on your laptop, and so do your folder and the tools.

Each time you send a message, the app sends the model everything it is to read: your message, the conversation so far, your `CLAUDE.md`, any file or image Claude has opened, and what the tools printed. The model writes text back. Anything that *happens* on your computer, like a file being saved or a tool running, is done by the app, on your laptop. (In the Chat tab, the "own space" where it runs code is far away too, on the provider's computers. Only the Code tab runs tools on your laptop.)

**Why it matters.**

- **Confidentiality.** A client's drawings, a competition entry under embargo, a tender price: if Claude opens the file, its contents are sent to the model's provider. Before you put project files in front of *any* AI app, check the AI policy of your school, your practice and your client.
- **No internet, no model.** The app opens, and the tools still run by themselves, but nothing can answer.
- **Limits.** Usage limits and costs are counted in the text that travels.

**Try it yourself.** Think of a file from a current project that you wouldn't email to a stranger. Would you open it with Claude? What would you want to check first?
