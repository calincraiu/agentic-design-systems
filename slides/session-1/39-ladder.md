# Today you climbed from asking to authoring

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="ladder"></div>

> **Bring one thing** you cared about in today's towers that no metric captured. Session 2 starts there.

<!-- notes -->
**The idea.** This module is a **ladder of authorship**: six steps from *using* an agent to *designing the system it works in*. You started on the bottom step, **prompting**: asking for things. Today you wrote the **context** (your `CLAUDE.md`), a **skill** of your own, and the first **contract** for a tool. Session 2 takes the tool further and adds **measures**: deciding what "good" means, in numbers. Later sessions connect it all into a **workflow**, with your decisions built in.

**Why it matters.** Each step up moves something from a vague request into something written down that you can read, change and test. The higher you climb, the more of the system is yours, and the less you have to take on trust. The question from the start of the session, *how would you know if any of this is good?*, is answered on the upper steps, not the lower ones.

**Before next time.** Write down one thing you cared about in today's towers that no metric captured: a view, a feeling at street level, a terrace. Bring it to Session 2. Keep your own folder as it is: your `CLAUDE.md`, skills and tools are in it. The course folder is replaced with a fresh copy each session, but your folder is yours and isn't touched.
