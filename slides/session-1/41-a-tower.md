# A tower

<p class="lead">From your words to the tool's numbers, and back.</p>

<div data-widget="figure" data-name="words-to-numbers" class="map"></div>

<!-- notes -->
Part 2 uses the same parts on a tower. The tower tool takes eight numbers and returns a tower with measurements. The harder step comes before the tool: turning what someone wants ("calm, but noticed from the river") into those eight numbers. The map shows that translation. Some words map onto a slider (*slender* becomes a width, *twisting* a twist), some don't map onto anything (*welcoming*), and the measurements that come back get turned into words again by the agent.

You play the client for a skill that does this translation. Then you write a **design stance** of your own, compare what it produces with your partner's, and set the result against Chat's answer from the start.
