# Two folders

<div class="cols" markdown="1">
<div class="box" markdown="1">

### The course folder

This app, the tools and the examples. You **run** it and **read** it, but you don't work in it.

It's replaced with a fresh copy each session. Anything you change here may disappear.

</div>
<div class="box yours" markdown="1">

### Your folder

Empty today. Everything you author goes here: your instructions, your skills, the tools you take away and the tools you design.

Nobody replaces it. **It's yours.**

</div>
