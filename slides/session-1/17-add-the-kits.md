# Add the kits

1. **Download** the zip below and **unzip** it.
2. **Copy** the `tools` and `.claude` folders into your own folder.
3. **Check** that both are there.

<!-- notes -->
A **kit** is a small folder of ready-made parts: tools, and the skills that use them. These three kits give you the add, dice and tower tools, and the `add-up`, `tie-breaker` and `tower-designer` skills. You use the tower kit in Part 2.

1. Download the zip from the link below the steps and unzip it. It contains two folders, `tools` and `.claude`.
2. Drag both into your own folder. If your folder already has a `.claude` folder, merge the two so nothing from either is lost.
3. Check that your folder now contains `tools/add.py`, `tools/dice.py`, `tools/tower/tower.py`, and `.claude/skills/add-up`, `.claude/skills/tie-breaker` and `.claude/skills/tower-designer`.

`.claude` is hidden, so if you can't see it, hidden items aren't switched on yet (previous slide; on a Mac, Cmd + Shift + . in Finder). It has to be copied, because that's where the skills are.

You can also copy straight from the course folder, where the kits are under `kits`. The widget shows their exact location on your computer. Copy the *contents* of `kits/add`, `kits/dice` and `kits/tower`, not the kit folders themselves.

The most common mistake is ending up with `tools` one level too deep, inside a folder left over from the unzip. The tools only run when `tools` sits directly in your folder. If you haven't finished when the class moves on, the next section only uses Chat and this app, so you can finish before you need your folder again.
