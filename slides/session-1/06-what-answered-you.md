You've just had an answer from Claude. The next three slides look at what produced it: where an LLM sits among the kinds of AI, the difference between the model and the app you use it through, and which parts run on your laptop. Most of what follows (the two tabs, tools, permission prompts) depends on telling the model and the app apart.

The names are easy to mix up. **Anthropic** is the company. **Claude** is its product, which has two parts: the **model**, which reads and writes text, and the **apps** you reach it through, such as the Claude desktop app or Claude Code. People say "Claude" for both. When you read "Claude did X", it's worth asking which of the two did it.
