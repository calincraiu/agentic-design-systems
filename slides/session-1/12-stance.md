# Write a design stance

The tower designer's stance is **Balanced**. Yours won't be.

1. **Take the tower kit** into your folder (prompt below).
2. Choose a stance and name it: *Good Neighbour* (the city first), *Sculptor* (silhouette leads), *Spreadsheet* (efficiency, always), *Storyteller* (the client's words, taken literally), or your own.
3. Ask Claude to copy `.claude/skills/tower-designer/` to `.claude/skills/tower-<your-stance>/`, and to change its name and description.
4. Rewrite the **Design stance** section yourselves. Leave the workflow alone. Say what it values, what it refuses, and how its three variants differ.
5. Start a **new session**, choose a card from `clients/`, and run your skill. One of you plays the client, **in character**.
6. Open `runs/gallery.html` and choose a direction yourselves.
