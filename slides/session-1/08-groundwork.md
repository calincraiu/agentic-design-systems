# Groundwork

<p class="lead">Where you'll work, what you'll write, and a folder of your own.</p>

<!-- notes -->
**In this section.** Five short slides before the parts of the system. They cover the two ways of working with Claude (the Chat tab and the Code tab), the kind of file everything in this course is written in (plain text), and the two folders you'll use (the course folder, which you only read, and your own folder, where you author). Then you make your own folder and copy the first two kits into it, so you can try every part of the system yourself as you meet it.

None of it is difficult, but every later slide depends on it. If you get lost later, it's usually because of one of these.
