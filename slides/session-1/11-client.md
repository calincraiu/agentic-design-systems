# Clients don't talk in eight numbers

<div class="cols" markdown="1">
<div data-widget="file" data-path="kits/tower/clients/the-needle.md" data-render="markdown" data-title="A client card"></div>
<div markdown="1">

Watch `/tower-designer` work on The Needle:

1. **Read** the card
2. **Write a brief**: facts, interpretations, assumptions, the contradiction
3. **Ask the client**, then *stop and wait*
4. **Generate** three different strategies with the tool
5. **Check** them against the limits
6. **Report** the trade-offs. *You* choose.

Getting from the card to the eight numbers is the design problem. The brief writes down how the card was read, so the reading can be questioned.
