# Session 1 in six steps

1. **What answered you?** The model, and the app around it.
2. **Groundwork.** Chat and Code, text files, a folder of your own.
3. **The parts.** LLM, tool, agent, skill, context.
4. **A tower.** From your words to numbers, and back.
5. **You are the client.** A brief, a stance, a crit.
6. **The ladder.** From asking to authoring.

<!-- notes -->
The first two steps are short and practical: what actually answered you when you used Claude, and getting your own folder ready. Step 3 is the longest. It introduces five parts of an agentic system one at a time (the LLM, the tool, the agent, the skill and context), each one added to fix something the previous part can't do on its own. Steps 4 and 5 put those parts to work on a tower, with you as the client. Step 6 places what you did today on a scale that the rest of the module follows.

To revise, write one sentence for each step from memory. The steps you can't write a sentence for are the ones to reread.
