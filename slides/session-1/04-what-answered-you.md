**In this section.** You've just had an answer from "Claude". Before the groundwork, three short slides on what actually answered you. They cover the family of things called AI and where an LLM sits in it, the difference between the **model** and the **app** around it, and which parts run on your laptop and which run far away.

These come first because almost everything later depends on them. The Chat tab and the Code tab, tools, skills, permission prompts and "check the receipt" all make more sense once you can tell the model apart from the app it runs in.
