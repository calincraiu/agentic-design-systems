# The Co-op

> "There are twenty-four of us, all retired, pooling our savings. Nobody wants to be more than a few floors from the garden. Everyone wants light. And we argue about everything, so whatever you do, it has to feel fair."

**On the card**
- Plot: 40 m east–west × 30 m north–south, with the garden on the south side.
- 24 flats of about 75 m² each, plus one shared room.
