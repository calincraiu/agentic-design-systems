# The Quiet Landmark

> "The city needs a landmark for the new riverside park: something people remember, something they give directions by. But the residents' association has been very clear. Nothing that shouts, and nothing that steals their afternoon sun."

**On the card**
- Plot: 25 × 25 m at the south edge of the park. The residents' terraces are directly to the north.
- Programme: a visitor centre and offices, about 5,000 m² in total.
