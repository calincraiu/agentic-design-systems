# The Lighthouse Keeper

> "I've kept the light on this coast for thirty-one years. The old tower is crumbling, and the harbour board wants a new one, one ships can see from far out at sea. They want me to live in it too, because who else would? Here's what nobody asks: I'm afraid of heights. I climb the stairs with my eyes shut."

**On the card**
- Plot: 18 × 18 m on the headland, cliff edge along the north side.
- The light must be visible from 40 km out at sea.
- Nothing may overhang the cliff.
- Home for one, plus a small room for visitors.
