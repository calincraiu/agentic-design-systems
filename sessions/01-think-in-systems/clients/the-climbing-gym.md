# The Climbing Gym

> "We're a climbing gym, and we want the building itself to be the climb. Overhangs! People should look up and think 'no way'. The bank, unfortunately, says it has to stand up and not cost a fortune."

**On the card**
- Plot: 25 × 25 m in an industrial area, with warehouses on every side.
- About 3,000 m² of floor in total.
