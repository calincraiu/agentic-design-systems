# The Vertical School

> "Our school has no land left. Six hundred children, one small plot, and they need playgrounds, lots of them, outdoors, on the building itself. And no child should ever feel they're in a skyscraper."

**On the card**
- Plot: 35 × 35 m in a dense neighbourhood.
- About 8 m² of floor per child.
- Outdoor play happens on the building (terraces count).
