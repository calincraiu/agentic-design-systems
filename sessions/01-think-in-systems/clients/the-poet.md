# The Poet

> "I want a tower that slowly turns its back on the city and looks at the mountains instead. Nothing dramatic. Slowly. The way someone loses interest in a conversation."

**On the card**
- Plot: 20 × 20 m. The old town lies to the south; the mountains are to the north-east.
- A writers' residence: 30 studios of about 40 m² each, plus shared rooms.
